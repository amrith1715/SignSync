ABOUT THE APPLICATION

About SignSync

A local workspace for exploring ASL fingerspelling.

From hand signs to letters

SignSync recognizes American Sign Language alphabet signs from your webcam and builds spelled text from confirmed letters. You can edit that text and read it aloud using your browser’s speech feature.

Three ways to explore

  • Recognition: spell letters live with your webcam and see the detected hand landmarks.
  • Testing Lab: try static sign images and videos of J or Z movements.
  • ASL Alphabet: browse the available hand sign references and descriptions.

Processed on your device

Camera frames and uploaded files are handled by the local recognition service. Image and video tests use the same recognition models as the camera.

What to expect

SignSync recognizes individual alphabet letters; it does not translate full ASL conversations. Lighting, hand position, framing and movement can affect results. Check the predicted letters before using the spelled text.
